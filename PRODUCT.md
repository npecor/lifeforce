# Product

## Register

product

## Users
ULC members (a longevity-clinic franchise) using the Ultimate Longevity Center mobile app. They open the **Labs tab** — a Lifeforce-owned iframe inside that app — to track one thing: where they are in their bloodwork journey, from booking a draw through results and clinician recommendations. They are health-motivated, often mid-task (just booked, awaiting results, reviewing a recommendation), and on a phone. They want orientation and the single clear next step, not exploration.

## Product Purpose
Render the Labs experience as a calm, trustworthy status surface across a seven-state journey: no draw → scheduled → processing → results back → post-clinician recommendations. Each state should answer "what's true now and what do I do next" at a glance. Actions that leave the tab are deep links into the surrounding ULC/Kite app (booking, cart, scheduling). Success = a member always knows their status and their next step without contacting support.

## Brand Personality
Evidence-led: **Scientific / Trustworthy / Cool.** Lead with specifics (dates, markers, names), not adjectives. No exclamation points. Spelling is "Lifeforce" (one word). Avoid hype vocabulary — no "unleash / biohack / journey / seamless / transform." Quiet confidence; the medicine and the data carry the weight.

## Anti-references
- Consumer wellness / biohacking apps that shout (gradients, hype copy, gamified streaks, exclamation points).
- Generic SaaS dashboards: hero-metric templates, identical card grids, decorative icon tiles repeated down the page.
- Anything that reads as marketing inside what is a clinical status surface.

## Design Principles
1. **One focal point per state.** Each screen has a single most-important fact (here: when/where the draw is) and one clear next action. Hierarchy makes it obvious; equal-weight rows hide it.
2. **Status before detail.** Lead with what's true now (scheduled, processing, ready), then the supporting specifics.
3. **Earned familiarity.** Standard, trusted affordances — real links, real buttons, a clear primary action. The tool disappears into the task.
4. **Specifics over decoration.** Icons and color carry meaning (status, action), never fill space. No repeated decorative tiles.
5. **The next step is never ambiguous.** Passive guidance reads as guidance; actions read as actions. They never look alike.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (avoid muted gold/gray on tinted near-white). Touch targets ≥44px. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Don't encode meaning in color alone — pair status color with a label or icon.
