Changelog

Prototype changelog

v0.12 · System fallback states + UI polish

  • Added: Fallback States — calm, plain-language screens for when the embedded Labs experience can't display: a loading skeleton, a connection-failure state ("We can't load your labs right now"), and a service-unavailable state ("Labs is temporarily unavailable"). Each reassures the member their lab data is safe and offers Try again plus a path to contact ULC.
  • Changed: Polished the scheduled-blood-draw card (green status bubble, distinct row icons, fixed "Get directions" link, title-only header) and redesigned the prescription selection checkbox (filled checked state with an animated check, clearer states, larger tap target).

v0.11 · Labs tab meeting updates

  • Added: Prescription recommendations now use checkbox multi-select, so a member can add several clinician-prescribed items to the Kite cart in one action. Items are checked by default, and the button shows how many will be added. This replaces the earlier per-item Buy/Subscribe button.
  • Added: A "View clinician note" button on the post-clinician recommendations screen, so members can read their clinician's note next to the recommendations.
  • Added: Redesigned the scheduled-blood-draw card. It now shows the in-center status, the appointment date and time, the fasting note, and the center's name and address with a "Get directions" link.
  • Added: Removed the old Recommendation Support Screens (Product Detail and Review Order). Product detail and checkout are owned by Kite, not the Labs tab.
  • Added: A quiet "Need help? Contact ULC." link at the bottom of every screen that deep-links to the ULC app's Contact Us page.
  • Added: An "Edge State Modules" library — a holding area for rare or conditional modules that are pulled into screens only when needed.
  • Added: A "Follow-up blood draw recommended" redraw module, housed in the Edge State Modules library. The trigger, member notification, and credit-redemption flow still need to be defined by the team.

v0.10 · Final review cleanup

  • Replaced the "Needs Ashley" and "Needs SprintFWD" labels with a single "Needs SprintFWD input" label on CTA destination notes.
  • Reorganized Open Questions into two clear groups: questions for SprintFWD and internal Lifeforce questions.
  • Removed older, granular open questions that the SprintFWD agenda already covers.
  • Removed the global CTA destination legend, but kept the per-screen CTA destination panels.
  • Removed the rigid "What happens next" timeline and kept the simpler Measure → Review → Personalize journey module.
  • Removed the locked/pending Recommendations screen from the core flow, since it isn't supported.
  • Removed the biomarker-specific "Recommended next steps" from the Biomarker Detail screen.
  • Removed the filter chips at the top of Lab Reports, but kept the Optimal / Normal / Out of Range summary chips on each report card.
  • Changed the in-progress Health Questionnaire to show percentage complete instead of which section you're on.
  • Reordered the Results Back / Questionnaire Incomplete screen so "Schedule Clinician Visit" is the top-priority button when it's available.
  • Removed the post-visit capsule from the post-clinician recommendations card.
  • Restored individual titles for the Recommendation Support Screens.

v0.9 · UX hierarchy cleanup

  • Made the single best next action clearer on each dashboard state.
  • Removed broad product browsing from the early states, keeping the no-labs screen focused on scheduling and the assessment.
  • Clarified the Labs journey conceptually, without forcing a rigid step-by-step sequence.
  • Reordered the results-back states so the Health Questionnaire and clinician scheduling appear in the requested priority.
  • Added a short clinical-rationale line to each prescription, supplement, and service recommendation.
  • Kept Documents lower in priority — after results, LifeScore, and recommendations.

v0.8 · June 09 review refinements

  • Renamed the first state to "No blood draw performed" and clarified the questionnaire state for a scheduled blood draw.
  • Removed the standalone processing screen and the broad catalog screens from the core all-screens flow.
  • Added LifeScore and Documents detail screens, based on the reference screenshots provided.
  • Expanded the LifeScore cards to include Quality of Life, Longevity Risk, and Biological Age.
  • Updated the post-clinician recommendations with separate prescription, supplement, and service sections.
  • Changed prescription CTAs to "Buy" and shifted supplements to "available at ULC" language.
  • Added Documents modules to the results-back and post-clinician states.

v0.7 · V0 state simplification

  • Reorganized the dashboard states around blood-draw and results status, rather than member vs. non-member.
  • Pared the no-labs screen down to orientation plus the scheduling actions that depend on deep links.
  • Moved post-clinician recommendations to the top of the post-visit state and split them into prescriptions, supplements, and ULC services.
  • Changed supplement recommendations to "Available at ULC" instead of in-app purchase language.
  • Added Documents as a lower-priority section, after recommendations, LifeScore, and lab reports.

v0.6 · Sticky CTA cleanup

  • Moved the recommendation, product, and order purchase buttons out of the scrolling content and into fixed bars at the bottom of the phone.
  • Anchored those purchase bars just above the bottom navigation so they feel intentional and consistent.
  • Added extra scroll padding on purchase screens so content can clear the bottom action bar.

v0.4 · Recommendation refinement

  • Simplified the post-clinician recommendation state to avoid repeating the same recommendation buttons.
  • Adjusted the post-clinician hierarchy (later superseded by the v0.7 recommendation-led structure).
  • Replaced the generic supplement examples with real prescription recommendations: Estradiol Patch and Semaglutide.
  • Moved Prescription Recommendations above LifeScore and Lab Reports in the post-clinician state.
  • Updated the labs/no-member card to use member-facing biomarker-testing language and removed the TBD chips from the mobile UI.

v0.3 · MVP state model pass

  • Updated the dashboard states to reflect the May 15 operational journey.
  • Added the labs/no-member and blood-draw-booked states.
  • Changed the blood-draw wording to "Schedule Blood Draw."
  • Hid the Health Questionnaire from the unscheduled member no-labs state.
  • Split the labs-processing state into questionnaire-incomplete and questionnaire-complete versions.
  • Removed the next-draw-due logic and Lifeforce membership management from the dashboard states.

v0.2 · Pre-MVP state update snapshot

  • Snapshot taken before the May 15 meeting-driven state model changes.
  • At this point the prototype includes the dashboard states, reports/results, and recommendations/products.
  • Kept as the baseline to revert to if the next round of changes goes in the wrong direction.
